T+01:11. You added TTL jitter. Cache hit rate 31% to 52%.

Root cause

A popular key expired together with thousands of its siblings. Every request missed at once and the database took the full read load.

The concept

Cache stampede: when many entries share one expiry, the cache stops absorbing traffic exactly when load is highest. Spread the expiry and the misses spread too.

Why that step hurt Harmful

Scaling the service added more callers for the same cold cache. The extra misses went straight to the database.

Incident Simulator teaches reliability patterns using the guides in ByteByteGo's system-design-101.
