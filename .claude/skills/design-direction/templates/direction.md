# Design direction

Version: 1 · Updated: YYYY-MM-DD · Status: current

## Product job
One sentence: what the person does here, and what they must see first.

## Tone
Two or three concrete words (for example: dense, calm, technical). Not "modern" or "clean".

## Type
Families, scale, weights, and why they fit this product.

## Color logic
How color carries meaning (status, emphasis, surface). Name the tokens, not hex values.

## Layout and density
Grid, spacing rhythm, how dense the main screen is and why.

## Motion
What moves, when, how long, and the reduced-motion fallback.

## Memorable detail
The one idea that makes this product feel intentional.

## Rejected
Directions considered and dropped, with the reason. Keep so they are not re-proposed.

## Reference basis
Ids from `references/` that shaped this version, and what was taken from each.
